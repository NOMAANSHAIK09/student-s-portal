About Student Portal

Empowering Students Through Technology

Student Portal is a modern educational platform designed to provide previous year question papers, study notes, syllabus, and useful academic resources in one place. Our mission is to help every student prepare smarter, save time, and perform better in exams.

OUR STORY

Built By Students, For Students.

We understand how difficult it can be for students to find reliable previous year question papers, study materials, and notes before examinations. Student Portal was created with a simple goal— make educational resources available anytime, anywhere.

Instead of searching through multiple WhatsApp groups, Google Drive folders, or asking seniors, students can access everything from one organized platform.
